Guitar players buy Neural DSP Archetypes the way they used to buy pedals: one for the clean tones, one for the high gain, one because a favourite player has a signature version. And just like pedals, some of them end up unused.

Neural DSP licenses can be resold, and the process runs entirely through iLok. Here's how.

## Can you resell a Neural DSP license?

Yes, if it's a **paid perpetual license**. Neural DSP licenses are managed on iLok, and the transfer happens in **iLok License Manager**. There's no need to contact Neural DSP support.

What can't be transferred:

- **Trials**
- **NFR licenses**
- **Some complimentary or promotional licenses**

Our database has a simple rule of thumb: if the transfer option isn't available for a license in iLok License Manager, that license isn't eligible.

## The 90-day rules

iLok adds two waiting periods of its own:

- A license **can't be transferred within 90 days** of being deposited into the account.
- A license **can't be transferred from an account less than 90 days old**.

If you just bought a plugin, or just created your iLok account, you'll have to wait.

## How much does it cost?

The cost is the **iLok transfer fee**: **$25 for one license or eligible bundle**, and **$50 maximum** for several licenses in one transaction. The **seller** pays it. Neural DSP doesn't add a fee of its own.

The $50 cap is worth knowing: if you're selling several Archetypes to the same buyer, transferring them together costs less than doing them one by one.

## How to transfer a Neural DSP license, step by step

1. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
2. **Get the buyer's iLok User ID.** If they don't have an iLok account yet, they create one for free (and remember the 90-day rule applies to the sender's account, not theirs).
3. **Deactivate the license** on your computers.
4. **Open iLok License Manager**, select the license and transfer it to the buyer's iLok User ID. Pay the fee there.
5. **Uninstall the plugin** on your side.

**Double-check the buyer's iLok User ID.** Transfers can't be reversed. A typo sends your license to a stranger.

## Traps to avoid

**Selling a promotional license.** Licenses you got free with something else may not be eligible. Check in iLok License Manager before you list.

**Selling too soon.** The 90-day rule catches people who buy during a sale and change their mind a month later.

**Getting the iLok ID wrong.** Copy-paste it, and ask the buyer to confirm it before you click.

**Forgetting the fee.** $25 per license is part of your cost. Selling several at once to the same buyer can bring it down.

## How to price a used Neural DSP plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price they can find, or they'll just buy it new.

Then adjust:

- **Add the iLok fee** to your math.
- **Name the exact Archetype** and version. Several have "X" versions and older editions.
- **Bundle deals** for buyers who want several: the $50 iLok cap makes it cheaper for you too.
