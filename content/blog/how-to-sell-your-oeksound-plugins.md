soothe became one of those plugins everyone seems to have on a vocal or a master bus. Spiff and bloom followed. And with soothe3 now out, a lot of soothe2 owners are wondering whether they can sell their old license.

oeksound has a clear resale policy, but its upgrade rules matter a lot. Here's how it works.

## Can you resell an oeksound license?

Yes. oeksound has an official page on reselling licenses. The conditions:

- **The license must be at least one month old.**
- **Transfers are final.** They can't be reverted once completed.
- **Rent-to-own licenses can't be transferred.**

## The upgrade rule: soothe2 and soothe3

This is where sellers get caught. oeksound's rules:

- **An upgrade license can't be sold by itself.** If you sell your soothe3 upgrade, both your soothe2 license and your soothe3 upgrade license are revoked, and the buyer receives a **full soothe3 license**.
- **If you own a soothe3 upgrade, you can't sell soothe2 on its own.**
- **Complimentary soothe / soothe2 licenses** that came with a soothe2 / soothe3 license are revoked as well.

In plain words: if you upgraded to soothe3, you can't keep soothe3 and sell soothe2. You sell the whole thing, and the buyer gets soothe3.

## How much does it cost?

oeksound charges **$15 per transfer (per license)**. The **seller** pays it, by buying a license transfer on oeksound.com.

## How to transfer an oeksound license, step by step

1. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
2. **Deactivate the license** on all your computers.
3. **Fill in the license transfer form** on oeksound.com (one transfer per license) and pay the fee.
4. **Follow the "Action required" email** you receive with the order confirmation, and confirm the transfer.
5. **oeksound checks and completes it.** It takes **one to two business days**.

## Traps to avoid

**Listing soothe2 when you own the soothe3 upgrade.** You can't sell soothe2 alone in that case. List what the buyer will actually get: a full soothe3 license.

**Selling a rent-to-own license.** Not transferable.

**Selling a license you bought last week.** Wait until it's at least a month old.

**Changing your mind.** Transfers are final. Make sure you're done with the plugin before you start.

## How to price a used oeksound plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price the buyer can find, or they'll just buy new.

Then adjust:

- **Add the $15 fee** to your math.
- **Price what the buyer receives.** If you sell a soothe2 + soothe3 upgrade, the buyer gets a full soothe3 license, so compare with soothe3's new price.
- **A plain soothe2 license** (no upgrade) is an older version. The buyer would pay again to upgrade, so price it below soothe3.
