Arturia's V Collection and FX Collection are on a lot of hard drives. So are the software bundles that came with a KeyLab or MiniLab. Between upgrades, new versions and hardware bundles, many users end up with Arturia licenses they don't use.

Arturia lets you resell them, and the process is simpler once you've owned the product for a while. Here's how it works.

## Can you resell an Arturia license?

Yes. Arturia's official help article explains how to unregister, resell and transfer a product to another user. How you do it depends on **how long the product has been registered on your account**:

- **Registered more than 6 months ago:** you can unregister it **yourself** from your Arturia account.
- **Registered less than 6 months ago:** you can't unregister it yourself. It has to go through **Arturia support**.

## How much does it cost?

Arturia's transfer instructions **don't mention any fee**. Our database used to list a small fee for licenses that were themselves bought second-hand, but it's no longer on Arturia's page. If you're unsure, ask Arturia before you agree on a price.

## How to transfer an Arturia license, step by step

**If the product was registered more than 6 months ago:**

1. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
2. **Log in to your Arturia account** and go to **My Products**.
3. **Click "Show more details"** next to the product and unregister it.
4. **The buyer registers the product** on their own Arturia account, with the serial number and unlock code.

**If it was registered less than 6 months ago:**

1. **Submit a ticket** through Arturia's support contact form.
2. **Choose "Registration & third party"** as the type of request, and select the product from the dropdown (that gives Arturia the serial number and unlock code automatically).
3. **Add the buyer's Arturia account email** in your description if you want Arturia to transfer the product directly to them, rather than just unregister it.
4. **Arturia processes the request**, and either unregisters the product or moves it to the buyer.

Arturia doesn't publish a delay for the support route. The self-service route is as quick as you are.

## The hardware bundle rule

If you're selling Arturia **hardware**, Arturia says **all bundled licenses transfer with it**, including any linked products you used to unlock a discounted upgrade path for that bundled software. So if you upgraded from a bundled license to a bigger product, selling the hardware can take that upgrade with it. Think about it before you list the hardware and the software separately.

## Traps to avoid

**Selling within 6 months without warning the buyer.** It works, but through a support ticket. Tell the buyer it won't be instant.

**Unregistering before you're paid.** Once it's out of your account, anyone with the serial number and unlock code can register it. Get paid first.

**Sending the codes in a public message.** Share the serial number and unlock code privately, and only once the deal is done.

**Splitting a hardware bundle.** Licenses bundled with hardware follow the hardware.

## How to price a used Arturia plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today, not the price you paid. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price the buyer can find.

Then adjust:

- **Version matters.** V Collection and FX Collection versions change what's inside. List the exact version you own.
- **No fee to cover**, as far as Arturia's page goes, which gives you some room.
