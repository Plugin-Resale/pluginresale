Addictive Drums 2 is a staple in home studios. RC-20 Retro Color shows up on half the lo-fi tracks out there, and XO has its fans for sorting drum samples. Some of these licenses came with an audio interface, some were bought outright, and plenty are no longer used.

XLN Audio makes resale free and fairly simple. Here's how it works, and the one check you need to do first.

## Can you resell an XLN Audio license?

Yes. XLN Audio allows license transfers **for all products, except NFR and OEM licenses**.

- **NFR** means Not For Resale.
- **OEM** means XLN software bundled with software or hardware from other companies, like **Focusrite, Novation and former Cakewalk** products.

The easy way to check: go to the **My Products** page of your XLN account. If a license is NFR or OEM, it has a **yellow label** saying so. **No label means you're free to transfer it.**

## The Addictive Drums upgrade rule

If you **upgraded Addictive Drums 1 licenses to Addictive Drums 2**, XLN says you can't transfer the AD1 and AD2 licenses separately. They go together.

## How much does it cost?

**Nothing.** Our database lists XLN Audio transfers as **free**.

## How to transfer an XLN Audio license, step by step

1. **Check the license type** in My Products: no yellow label = transferable.
2. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
3. **Uninstall the products** you're selling with the XLN Online Installer.
4. **Remove your computer registration(s)** in your XLN account (My Account > Computers).
5. **Ask the buyer to create an XLN Audio account** if they don't have one.
6. **Contact XLN Audio support** with the buyer's XLN account and the list of products to transfer.
7. **XLN moves the licenses** to the buyer's account and confirms it.
8. **Re-register your computer(s)** with the XLN Online Installer afterwards, if you keep other XLN products.

XLN doesn't publish a delay. It depends on support.

## Traps to avoid

**Selling an interface bundle license.** If your Addictive Drums came with a Focusrite or Novation interface, it's probably OEM and can't be transferred. Check for the yellow label.

**Selling AD2 while keeping AD1 (or the other way round)** when you upgraded. They have to go together.

**Forgetting your other products.** Removing your computer registration affects your XLN products on that machine. Re-register it once XLN confirms the transfer.

## How to price a used XLN Audio plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today, not the price you paid. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price they can find, or they'll just buy it new.

Then adjust:

- **No fee to cover**: the transfer is free, which gives you room on the price.
- **List exactly what's included.** If your license includes expansions, name them. The buyer will want to know.
