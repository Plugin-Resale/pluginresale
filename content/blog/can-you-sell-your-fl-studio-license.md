FL Studio is one of the most popular DAWs in the world, especially among beatmakers. Producer Edition, Signature Bundle, All Plugins Edition: plenty of people own one. And some of them switch DAW and wonder whether their FL Studio license can be sold.

Here's Image-Line's answer, and its reasons.

## Can you resell an FL Studio license?

**No.** Image-Line's knowledge base says its End User License Agreement (**Section 11**) doesn't allow license transfers. You can't sell, give away or transfer an **FL Studio or plugin license** to anyone else. **Sharing your license or your account** is also forbidden, and our database notes it can get the license revoked.

That's why Image-Line products can't be listed on Plugin Resale: we only list licenses the developer will move to the buyer.

## Why Image-Line says no

Image-Line explains it openly, and it's tied to one of FL Studio's best-known features: **Lifetime Free Updates**.

- **Free updates and transfers don't mix.** If licenses could be transferred, a second, third or tenth owner would still get every future version of FL Studio for free.
- **The value never drops.** Because of free updates, a used FL Studio license would keep its value indefinitely, unlike other software that ages.
- **Every transferred license is a lost sale**, which Image-Line says competes directly with its main business.

Image-Line presents it as a deal: lifetime free updates, in exchange for not selling or sharing the license. It says it has stood by that since 1998.

## Why a "used FL Studio" is a risky buy

Since Image-Line doesn't transfer licenses, a seller can't put FL Studio in your name. What's on offer is usually a shared account, which Image-Line forbids and can revoke. You'd pay for a license you don't own, which can stop working at any time.

## What about EU law?

In the EU, the law may allow the resale of software bought outright even when a license forbids it. But Image-Line won't move the license to the buyer, so the buyer would have to go to court to use it. Our article on [whether you can legally resell your plugins](/blog/can-i-legally-resell-my-plugins) explains the legal side.

## What can you do with an FL Studio license you don't use?

- **Keep it.** With lifetime free updates, it stays current if you ever come back.
- **Check your other software.** Many DAW makers do allow transfers. Our [DAW guide](/blog/selling-a-daw-license) compares their rules.
