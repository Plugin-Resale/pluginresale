Output instruments and effects have a very recognizable sound: Portal for granular textures, Movement for rhythmic motion, Analog Strings and Substance for cinematic and bass sounds. Many producers bought a few, then moved on.

Output does allow resale, but with an unusual rule about where you live. Here's how it works.

## Can you resell an Output license?

Yes, **only if both the buyer and the seller are based in the EU or UK**. Output's EULA allows transfers in that case, with both parties' billing address in the EU or UK.

If either of you is outside the EU and UK, a transfer isn't possible under Output's terms.

What can't be sold:

- **Arcade**, which is a subscription.
- **Audio rendered from Output instruments and effects** can never be resold as a sample or loop pack. That's about the sounds, not the license, but it's part of the same EULA.

## How much does it cost?

**Nothing.** Our database lists Output transfers as **free**.

## How to transfer an Output license, step by step

1. **Check that you and the buyer are both in the EU or UK** (billing address). Ask the buyer before you agree on anything.
2. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
3. **Ask for the buyer's Output account.** They need one to receive the license.
4. **Move the license** to the buyer's Output account.
5. **Uninstall the product** on your side.

Our database doesn't list a typical delay.

## Traps to avoid

**Selling to a buyer outside the EU or UK.** That's the main trap. Say "EU/UK buyers only" clearly in your listing.

**Trying to sell Arcade.** It's a subscription, not a license you own.

**Mixing up products.** Output sells instruments, effects and expansions (like Rev X-Loops). Name the exact product.

## How to price a used Output plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today, not the price you paid. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price they can find, or they'll just buy new.

Then adjust:

- **No fee to cover**: the transfer is free.
- **Smaller pool of buyers**: only EU and UK buyers can take it. A slightly lower price helps it sell.

Our [pricing guide](/blog/how-much-is-my-used-plugin-worth) walks through the full method with an example.
