Softube makes some of the most respected emulations around: Tube-Tech, Trident, Weiss, Summit Audio, plus Console 1 for people who like to mix on hardware controls. Between bundles and individual purchases, many Softube users own licenses they no longer open.

Softube licenses can be resold through iLok. Here's how, and the bundle rule to watch.

## Can you resell a Softube license?

Yes. Softube licenses are transferred through **iLok License Manager**. Two rules:

- **A license can't be transferred until 90 days after the original deposit.**
- **Plugins that are part of locked bundles can't be sold separately.** Softube names Volume 6, Weiss Complete Collection and Empirical Labs Complete Collection as examples, and says it applies to **the majority of its bundles**.

## How much does it cost?

The cost is iLok's fee: **$25 per license or bundle transferred**, with **$50 maximum per order**. Softube doesn't mention a fee of its own.

If you're selling several licenses to the same buyer, transferring them in one order keeps the cost down.

## How to transfer a Softube license, step by step

1. **Check the 90-day rule**, and whether the license is part of a locked bundle.
2. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
3. **Get the buyer's iLok User ID.** If they don't have one, they create a free iLok account.
4. **Install and open iLok License Manager**, and sign in with your iLok account.
5. **Right-click the license(s)** you're selling and choose **Transfer License**. Enter the buyer's iLok User ID and pay the fee.
6. **Uninstall the plugin** on your side.

## The auto-created iLok account catch

If your iLok account was **created automatically by Softube's system**, you may not have a password for it. Softube says to use **"Forgot password"** when logging into iLok License Manager to set one, before you can transfer anything.

## Traps to avoid

**Selling one plugin out of a locked bundle.** Most Softube bundles are locked. If yours is, sell the whole bundle or nothing.

**Selling too early.** 90 days after deposit, not before.

**Typing the wrong iLok ID.** Ask the buyer to confirm it, and copy-paste it.

**Doing several transfers one by one.** If you're selling several licenses to the same buyer, one order caps the fee at $50.

## How to price a used Softube plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today, not what you paid. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price they can find, or they'll just buy new.

Then adjust:

- **Add the iLok fee** to your math.
- **Bundles** are priced against the bundle's own new price.
- **Name the exact product** (for example Tube-Tech CL 1B, Tape, Console 1...), so the buyer knows what lands in their iLok.
