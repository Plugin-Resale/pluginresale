Eventide plugins have a way of piling up. Blackhole comes in a bundle, H3000 Factory gets bought during a sale, and before long the Anthology XII collection sits next to three individual plugins you already owned.

Eventide licenses can be resold, and the process is one of the simplest around because it's all done in iLok. Here's how.

## Can you resell an Eventide license?

Yes. Eventide licenses live on iLok, and you transfer them to the buyer's iLok account through **iLok License Manager**. Eventide doesn't need to be involved.

Our database doesn't list any Eventide-specific restriction. iLok's general rules still apply, like the fact that a transfer can't be undone once it's done. See our [iLok guide](/blog/ilok-license-transfer-explained) for the details.

## How much does it cost?

Eventide doesn't charge its own fee. The cost is the **$25 iLok transfer fee**, charged by PACE (the company behind iLok). The **seller** pays it in iLok License Manager.

## How to transfer an Eventide license, step by step

1. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
2. **Get the buyer's iLok User ID.** If they don't have an iLok account yet, they create one for free.
3. **Deactivate the license** on your computers or iLok key.
4. **Open iLok License Manager**, select the license and transfer it to the buyer's iLok User ID. Pay the $25 fee there.
5. **Uninstall the plugin** on your side.

Our database lists the transfer as **immediate**: the license shows up in the buyer's iLok account as soon as you complete it.

## Traps to avoid

**Typing the wrong iLok ID.** iLok transfers can't be reversed. Ask the buyer to confirm their iLok User ID in writing, and copy-paste it.

**Transferring before you're paid.** Since the transfer is instant and final, get paid first.

**Forgetting the fee.** $25 is a big chunk of the price of a cheaper plugin. Include it in your numbers.

## How to price a used Eventide plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today, not the price you paid. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price the buyer can find, or they'll just buy it new.

Then adjust:

- **Add the $25 iLok fee** to your math. On a low-priced plugin, it sets a clear floor.
- **Bundles** are priced against the bundle's own new price, not the sum of the plugins.
- **Name the exact product**, so the buyer knows what's coming to their iLok.

Our [pricing guide](/blog/how-much-is-my-used-plugin-worth) walks through the full method with an example.

Plugin Resale is free to list and free to buy, with no commission. The buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services, and every Eventide listing shows the transfer rules right next to the price.
