Pro Tools is still the standard in a lot of studios, and many engineers own a perpetual license. If you've switched DAW, or moved to a subscription, that perpetual license could be worth something to someone else.

Avid has an official Transfer of Ownership process. Here's how it works.

## Can you resell a Pro Tools license?

Yes, if it's a **perpetual license**. Avid's end-user license terms allow a **one-time permanent transfer** of the license, through Avid's Transfer of Ownership process.

What can't be transferred:

- **Subscriptions.** Only perpetual licenses can be transferred.
- **Academic licenses** and **NFR / evaluation licenses**, except where the law requires it.
- **A license already used for an upgrade**, separately from the upgraded version. The upgraded license goes as a whole.

The same process covers other Avid software in our catalogue, like perpetual **Sibelius** licenses.

## How much does it cost?

Avid charges a **Transfer of Ownership fee**. The exact amount is set by Avid and paid through **avid.com/avidlicensing**. Check the current fee there before you agree on a price with the buyer.

## How to transfer a Pro Tools license, step by step

1. **Check that your license is perpetual**, not a subscription, and not academic or NFR.
2. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
3. **Submit a Transfer of Ownership request** through avid.com/avidlicensing (or through Avid Customer Care). It has to come from the **original licensee**.
4. **Pay the transfer fee.**
5. **Give up every copy of the software** on your side, as Avid's terms require.
6. **Avid reassigns the license** to the buyer.

Our database doesn't list a typical delay. It depends on Avid.

## Traps to avoid

**Selling a subscription.** Not transferable, even if you've paid for years.

**Selling a license that was already transferred.** Avid allows a one-time transfer. If you bought it used, it can't be sold again.

**Splitting an upgrade.** If you upgraded, the whole upgraded license goes together.

**Keeping a copy installed.** Avid's terms say you give up every copy.

## How to price a used Pro Tools license

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today, and remember that many buyers compare a perpetual license with the cost of a subscription. A used perpetual license has to be clearly cheaper than buying new, or they'll just buy new.

Then adjust:

- **Factor in Avid's fee.** Check the current amount before you set your price.
- **Edition and version:** Studio or Ultimate, and the version the license covers. Name them exactly.
- **One-time transfer:** the buyer will never be able to resell it. That's worth a discount.
