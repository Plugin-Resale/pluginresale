iZotope's tools are everywhere in mixing and mastering: Ozone, Neutron, RX, Nectar and more. They also come up often on the second-hand market, because many people own a bundle or an older version they've replaced.

iZotope allows license transfers, doesn't charge for them, and handles them through its support team. There are a few rules to know before you sell or buy.

## Can you transfer an iZotope license?

Yes, if it's a **full license that was bought**. iZotope excludes:

- **time-limited licenses** (licenses that expire, such as trials),
- **educational licenses**,
- **NFR licenses** (Not For Resale),
- **licenses bundled with retail products or hardware**.

## How much does it cost?

iZotope itself charges **nothing** for a transfer. If the product was activated with **iLok**, PACE's iLok transfer fee applies (currently $25). Our [iLok guide](/blog/ilok-license-transfer-explained) explains how that works.

## How to transfer an iZotope license

The transfer has to be requested by the **original owner** of the license:

1. **Contact iZotope's Account Support team** and ask to transfer your license to another person.
2. Give them the **buyer's details** (name and the email address of their iZotope account) and the product you're transferring.
3. **Deactivate the product** on your computers.
4. iZotope moves the license to the buyer's account.

Only the **current version** of the product is transferred. Past versions attached to that license are disabled. If you own Ozone 12 and it's an upgrade from Ozone 11, the buyer gets Ozone 12, and you can't keep using Ozone 11.

## The "once only" rule

iZotope doesn't allow a second transfer of a product you've already transferred. For example, you can't buy a product, transfer it to someone, buy the same product again and transfer that one too.

Keep this in mind as a buyer too: before paying, ask whether the license is being transferred for the first time.

## Tips for sellers

- **Check your license type** in your iZotope account: a license that came with a hardware product or at a student price won't qualify.
- **Say which version it is** in your listing, and whether it's a standalone product or part of a bundle.
- **Tell the buyer about the iLok fee** if it applies, and agree on who pays it.

## Tips for buyers

- **Create an iZotope account** before buying, and give the seller the exact email address you used.
- **Ask for the version** and check what upgrading to the latest one costs. iZotope regularly runs upgrade deals, which affect what a used license is worth.
- **Don't confirm the deal** until the product appears in your iZotope account and authorizes.
