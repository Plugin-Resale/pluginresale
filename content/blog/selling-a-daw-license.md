Switching DAWs is common: you start on one, a collaborator or a course pulls you towards another, and the old license just sits there. Since a full DAW license often costs several hundred euros, it's worth knowing whether you can sell it.

The answer depends entirely on the publisher. Some make it a two-minute job, others don't allow it at all.

## Ableton Live: free and self-service

Ableton has one of the easiest transfer systems around. The seller clicks **Transfer ownership** next to the license in their Ableton account and enters the buyer's email. The buyer accepts it from their own account. It's **free** and immediate.

Restrictions: **NFR licenses can't be transferred**, and **educational licenses** can only go to another eligible student or teacher.

## Cubase and Nuendo (Steinberg): deactivate, then request

Steinberg licenses can be resold. The seller first **deactivates the license on every computer** in Steinberg Activation Manager, then sends Steinberg a **change of ownership request**, with details that match their MySteinberg account.

Restrictions: **educational licenses** are personal and can't be sold, and **OEM versions** bundled with hardware (Cubase AI, LE, or Elements when it came with a device) can't be sold separately from that hardware.

## Pro Tools (Avid): perpetual licenses only

Avid has a **Transfer of Ownership** program for perpetual Pro Tools licenses. The original owner submits a request through Avid's licensing site or Avid Customer Care and gives up every copy of the software; Avid then reassigns the license to the buyer. Avid charges a **transfer fee**.

Restrictions:

- **Subscriptions** can't be transferred, only perpetual licenses.
- A license can be transferred **only once**, permanently.
- **Academic** and **NFR** licenses are excluded.
- A license that was used to **upgrade** can't be sold separately from the upgraded version.

## Bitwig Studio: by email

The seller emails **Bitwig support** and asks to transfer the license to the buyer's account, giving the buyer's name, username and country. The buyer needs a Bitwig account first.

Restrictions: **NFR** licenses can't be transferred. **Educational** and **crossgrade** licenses can move only if the new owner proves they're eligible.

## Digital Performer (MOTU): through customer service

The registered owner contacts **MOTU Customer Service** to transfer ownership, with both their own and the buyer's registration details and signed documentation. Once you've upgraded, you can't sell the previous version on its own.

## Reason: the DAW yes, the Rack Extensions no

The **Reason** license itself can be transferred to another user's account. The **Rack Extensions** you bought as add-ons, however, stay with the original account and can't be transferred.

## REAPER: ask first

Cockos' license agreement doesn't formally allow resale, but the company has said informally it's fine with a license being resold if you ask first. Email Cockos **before** buying or selling a used REAPER license.

## Studio Pro (Fender, formerly PreSonus Studio One): self-service, $25

Studio One was renamed **Fender Studio Pro** in January 2026, and the transfer program carried over. The buyer creates a Fender account first. The seller logs in to **my.fender.com**, opens the product under **My Gear**, clicks **Transfer License**, enters the buyer's account email and pays the **$25 transfer fee** ($5 per add-on). The transfer goes through as soon as the fee is paid, and both sides get a confirmation email: keep it.

Restrictions:

- **NFR** licenses and software that came **free with hardware** can't be transferred, and neither can Prime, demo or beta versions.
- A **perpetual license left over from a yearly subscription** can't be transferred.
- Licenses linked by an **upgrade** move together, as one license.
- Bundled third-party software such as **Melodyne** isn't part of the transfer: it has to be moved separately with its own developer.

## The ones you can't sell

- **FL Studio (Image-Line)**: Image-Line's license agreement forbids selling, giving away or transferring an FL Studio or plugin license. It's the flip side of its famous lifetime free updates.
- **Logic Pro (Apple)**: it's sold only on the Mac App Store and tied to your Apple ID. Apple app licenses can't be transferred.

## Before you buy a used DAW

- **Check the version** and what the upgrade to the latest one costs. DAW publishers often discount upgrades heavily, which affects what a used license is worth ([our pricing guide](/blog/how-much-is-my-used-plugin-worth)).
- **Check the edition**: Intro, Standard and Suite, or Elements, Artist and Pro, don't have the same value.
- **Make sure it's not an OEM version** that came with an audio interface or a controller.
- **Create your account** with the publisher before the deal, so the transfer can start right away.
