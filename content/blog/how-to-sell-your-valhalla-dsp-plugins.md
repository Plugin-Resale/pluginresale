Valhalla plugins are the reverbs and delays that show up in almost every mix template. VintageVerb, Room, Delay, Shimmer, Plate: they're affordable, great-sounding, and that's why so many people own all of them. If you've settled on one or two, the others could go to someone else.

Valhalla allows transfers. Here's how it works right now.

## Can you resell a Valhalla DSP license?

Yes. Valhalla DSP lets you transfer a license to someone else. Two points to know:

- **Automatic transfers are temporarily disabled.** Valhalla says it switched them off to work out some technical issues with its website. In the meantime, you **contact Valhalla through its support form** to transfer a license.
- **Only licenses bought on valhalladsp.com** count. Valhalla says it only sells through its own website, and that any sale that doesn't involve creating a Valhalla DSP account isn't authorized.

## How much does it cost?

Neither our database nor Valhalla's FAQ mentions a fee.

## How to transfer a Valhalla DSP license, step by step

1. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
2. **Ask the buyer to create a Valhalla DSP account** if they don't have one. They need it to receive the license.
3. **Contact Valhalla through its support form** and ask for the transfer, with the buyer's details.
4. **Valhalla moves the license** to the buyer's account.
5. **Uninstall the plugin** on your side.

Since the transfer goes through support, there's no published delay. Tell the buyer it's handled by Valhalla, not instant.

## A warning for buyers

Valhalla is blunt about one thing: it has seen people selling Valhalla plugins on eBay, and says they have all been **unauthorized copies** so far. Any authorized sale requires the buyer to have a **Valhalla DSP user account**, and Valhalla won't support plugins bought through unauthorized third parties.

In other words: a legit used Valhalla license ends up in your own Valhalla account, moved by Valhalla. Anything else, walk away.

## Traps to avoid

**Selling a license you didn't buy from Valhalla.** If it didn't come through valhalladsp.com and a Valhalla account, it isn't a license Valhalla will transfer.

**Sending a license file or login.** The buyer needs the license in their own Valhalla account.

**Promising an instant transfer.** For now, it goes through Valhalla's support.

## How to price a used Valhalla plugin

Valhalla plugins are already affordable new, so a used license has a smaller gap to play with. It has to be clearly cheaper than buying new, or the buyer won't bother.

Then adjust:

- **Group them.** Several Valhalla plugins in one deal are more interesting to a buyer than one cheap license, and it's one support request instead of several.
- **Name each plugin exactly**, so the buyer knows what goes into their account.
