Auto-Tune is probably the most famous audio plugin name in the world. Auto-Tune Pro, Artist, EFX+, Access, Harmony Engine: there are plenty of versions around, and plenty of people who bought one and no longer use it.

So can you sell it? Here's what Antares says.

## Can you resell an Auto-Tune license?

**No.** Antares' official Transfer of Ownership Policy says that, under **Section 5 (License Grant; Restrictions)** of the Antares Software License Agreement, **Antares licenses are non-transferable**.

The agreement also says you must not make the software available to any other person, including by sublicensing, distributing or transferring it.

That's why Antares products can't be listed on Plugin Resale: we only list licenses the developer will move to the buyer.

## Where to read the agreement

Antares says a copy of its Software License Agreement is installed on your computer with any of its products:

- **Mac:** /Library/Documentation/Antares Audio Technologies
- **Windows:** C:\Program Files\Antares Audio Technologies

## Why a "used Auto-Tune" is a risky buy

Since Antares doesn't transfer licenses, a seller can't put Auto-Tune in your name. What's usually on offer is a shared account login, or a license that stays registered to the seller. In both cases, you pay for something you don't own, and it can stop working at any time.

If someone offers you a "used Auto-Tune license", ask how Antares will move it to your account. Under Antares' own policy, it won't.

## What about EU law?

In the EU, the law may allow the resale of software bought outright even when a license forbids it. But in practice, Antares won't move the license to the buyer, so the buyer would have to go to court to use it. Our article on [whether you can legally resell your plugins](/blog/can-i-legally-resell-my-plugins) explains the legal side.

## What can you do with an Auto-Tune license you don't use?

- **Keep it.** It's still yours, and vocal tuning comes back in almost every project eventually.
- **Look at the rest of your plugin folder.** Many other vocal and mixing tools come from developers that do allow transfers.
