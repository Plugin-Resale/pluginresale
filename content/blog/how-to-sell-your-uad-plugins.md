If you've worked on an Apollo for a few years, you probably own a serious UAD library: 1176s, Pultecs, a couple of tape machines, maybe a Neve channel strip or two. Then you switch interfaces, or move to a native-only setup, and the question comes up: can I sell those plugins?

Short answer: not on their own. Here's how Universal Audio's rules actually work, and what you can do instead.

## Can you resell UAD plugin licenses?

**Not separately.** Universal Audio doesn't let you sell a UAD plugin license by itself. UAD DSP plugins are tied to the Apollo, Satellite or UAD-2 hardware they're registered to, and they can only move **together with that hardware**.

That's why UAD plugins can't be listed on Plugin Resale: we only list licenses that can be transferred on their own.

## How the hardware transfer works

When you sell an Apollo, Satellite or UAD-2 card, UA lets you choose between two options:

- **Transfer the hardware with all your UAD DSP plugins.** Every UAD DSP plugin license in the system tied to that device goes to the new owner, plus the associated **perpetual** UAD Native (UADx) licenses.
- **Transfer the hardware without the plugins.** All plugin licenses stay in your account.

There's no middle ground. UA is explicit: **individual plugins can't be selected for transfer**. It's your whole library, or none of it.

The current owner starts the transfer from their UA account ("Transfer a Device"). If you choose to include the plugins and you have UADx licenses activated, you have to deactivate them in iLok License Manager first. The device stays "pending" in your account until the new owner registers it.

Good to know for the buyer: Apollo, Satellite and UAD-2 accelerators registered as used come with a collection of plugins free of charge, whether or not the seller transfers their library.

## What about UADx native plugins?

Perpetual UADx licenses move only when they're linked to a UAD DSP plugin in the transferred library. **UADx licenses without a UAD DSP version can't be transferred at all.** If you bought native-only UADx plugins, there's no official way to resell them.

## Traps to avoid

**Buying "UAD plugins" from someone who keeps the hardware.** Since licenses only move with the device, a seller offering plugins alone can't deliver them through UA. Walk away.

**Forgetting the rest of your system.** If the device belongs to a system with other UAD devices and you transfer the plugin library, UA says all devices in that system must be re-authorized for the transfer to complete.

**Registering the device before the transfer.** UA says a transfer can't be started once the hardware is registered to a different account. The seller starts the transfer first, then the buyer registers.

**Selling the hardware cheap and forgetting the library.** If your Apollo carries a big UAD library, transferring it with the unit adds real value. Price the bundle accordingly, or keep the plugins in your account and sell the hardware alone.

## What are your options?

1. **Sell the hardware with the full library.** It's the only official way to pass your UAD DSP plugins on.
2. **Sell the hardware alone** and keep the plugins in your account, in case you come back to UAD hardware later.
3. **Keep everything.** If you still use some of the plugins, there's no way to sell the ones you don't.

Hardware sales happen on regular gear marketplaces. Plugin Resale is for software licenses that can be transferred on their own.
