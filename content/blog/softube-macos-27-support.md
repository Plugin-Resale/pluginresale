Softube released version **2.6.47** of its plugins on **3 November 2026**. Two lines in the release notes matter to anyone on a Mac:

- **macOS 27 Golden Gate is now officially supported.**
- **Support for macOS Sonoma 14 is now deprecated.**

Both are listed under "All plug-ins", so they cover the whole range: Tube-Tech, Weiss, Trident, Chandler Limited and the rest. The other changes are two bug fixes for Flow Controls, in Flow Mixing Suite, Flow Mastering Suite and Flow Studio.

## I'm on Sonoma 14. Do my Softube plugins stop working?

No. Deprecated doesn't mean removed, and Softube gives no end date. What you have installed today keeps working.

Deprecated usually means the developer no longer tests new releases on that version. So a future Softube update may stop installing on Sonoma, and a bug that only happens there may not get fixed. If you plan to stay on Sonoma for a while, keep a copy of the installers that work for you.

None of this is a surprise: Sonoma came out in 2023, and Apple has released Sequoia, Tahoe and now macOS 27 since.

## Buying a used Softube license

A license you buy used is the same license the first owner had, with the same downloads. For a plugin Softube still sells, that means the current version, macOS 27 support included.

One thing to check: older products Softube has replaced. The original Tube-Tech CL 1B, for example, has been succeeded by the CL 1B Mk II, and some plugins are still sold used years after they left Softube's shop. Before you buy one, ask the seller which version they run and on which macOS.

## Selling a used Softube license

Say it in your listing. "Current version, runs on macOS 27" answers a question the buyer would ask anyway.

The transfer rules don't change with this update:
