A lot of Slate Digital users today are on the All Access subscription. But plenty of engineers bought perpetual licenses before that, or picked up a single plugin like FG-X or the Virtual Mix Rack. If one of those is sitting unused, you can sell it.

Here's how a Slate Digital transfer works, and the one thing that decides whether your license is sellable at all.

## Can you resell a Slate Digital license?

Yes, **if it's a license you own**. Slate Digital licenses live on iLok, and Slate lets you transfer them to another user through iLok License Manager.

The exception: **licenses included in the All Access subscription can't be transferred.** You're renting those, not owning them. If you're an All Access member, only the licenses you bought outright are sellable.

## How much does it cost?

Slate Digital says it **doesn't charge any additional fee**. The cost is the iLok transfer fee charged by PACE (the company behind iLok), which our database lists at **$25**. The **seller** pays it.

## How to transfer a Slate Digital license, step by step

1. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
2. **Get the buyer's iLok User ID.** If they don't have an iLok account yet, they create one for free.
3. **Deactivate the license** on your machines.
4. **Open iLok License Manager** and sign in with your iLok ID.
5. **Select the license** you're selling and click the **Transfer** icon in the top right corner. Enter the buyer's iLok User ID and pay the iLok fee.
6. **Contact Slate Digital support** if the buyer wants the product to show up in their Slate Digital account and in the Connect app. Slate says it will help with a full transfer that includes account integration.
7. **Uninstall the plugin** on your side.

The iLok transfer happens as soon as you confirm it. Slate doesn't publish a delay for the account integration part.

## Traps to avoid

**Trying to sell an All Access license.** If your plugin came with the subscription, it isn't yours to sell. Check in iLok License Manager which licenses you actually bought.

**Skipping the Slate account step.** The license works once it's in the buyer's iLok account, but the buyer may also want it in their Slate account and in Connect. Tell the buyer upfront that you'll contact Slate support for that part.

**Sending a serial or a login.** A Slate license should land in the buyer's own iLok account. Never share your iLok or Slate login.

**Forgetting the fee.** $25 matters on a cheaper plugin. Put it in your numbers before you set the price.

## How to price a used Slate Digital plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today, and keep in mind that many buyers compare a perpetual license with the cost of a subscription. A used perpetual license has to be clearly cheaper than buying new, or they'll just buy new.

Then adjust:

- **Add the iLok fee to your math.** It sets a floor on how low you can go.
- **Say it's perpetual.** Buyers who don't want a subscription are exactly the people looking for a used Slate license. Make it obvious in your listing.
- **Name the exact product** (Virtual Mix Rack, FG-X 2, Virtual Tape Machines...) so the buyer knows what they're getting.
