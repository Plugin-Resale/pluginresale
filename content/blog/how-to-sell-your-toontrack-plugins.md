Toontrack is one of those developers people collect without noticing. EZdrummer first, then a few EZX expansions, then Superior Drummer, and maybe EZkeys and EZbass on top. If you've moved on to Superior Drummer, that old EZdrummer license could go to someone just starting out.

Toontrack allows transfers, with a small fee per serial. Here's how it works.

## Can you resell a Toontrack license?

Yes. Toontrack lets you transfer ownership of a serial number (a license transfer), with two conditions:

- **The serial must have been registered to your account for 30+ days.**
- **You can't sell partial licenses**, or any authorization separately from the license it belongs to.

The buyer also takes on Toontrack's End-User License Agreement: Toontrack asks the seller to tell the new owner that the product and license remain subject to it.

## How much does it cost?

Toontrack charges an **administration fee of USD 15 / EUR 15 / SEK 150 per serial number**. If you're selling a core product plus several expansions, each serial counts.

## How to transfer a Toontrack license, step by step

1. **Check the 30-day rule** for each serial you're selling.
2. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
3. **Log in to your Toontrack account.**
4. **Contact Toontrack support** from its FAQ page and choose **"License Transfer"** as the issue type.
5. **Pay the administration fee** for each serial number.
6. **Remove the product** from your computer, server and backup devices, or make it unusable.
7. **Tell the buyer** that the product and license remain subject to Toontrack's EULA.

Toontrack doesn't publish a delay. It depends on support.

## Traps to avoid

**Forgetting that fees add up.** A core product plus five EZX expansions is six serials, so six fees. Include them all in your price.

**Selling a serial registered last week.** Wait 30 days.

**Keeping a copy.** Toontrack requires the product to be removed from your computers and backups. Do it.

**Splitting a license.** You can't sell part of a license, or an authorization on its own.

## How to price a used Toontrack product

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price they can find, or they'll just buy it new.

Then adjust:

- **Add the fees:** $15 per serial number.
- **Group expansions with the core product** when it makes sense. A buyer starting out may prefer one bundle deal to five separate listings.
- **List the exact product and version** (EZdrummer, Superior Drummer...) and every expansion included.
