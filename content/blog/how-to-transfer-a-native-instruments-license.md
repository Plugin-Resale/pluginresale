Native Instruments makes some of the most popular instruments and bundles in music production: Komplete, Kontakt, Massive, Battery, Guitar Rig and many more. Komplete in particular is a big purchase, and a lot of people end up owning a version they've outgrown or never fully used.

The good news: NI has one of the simplest transfer systems out there. The seller creates a code, the buyer imports it, and that's it.

## How the NI transfer works

NI uses a **Transfer ID**: a long code (it looks like `86b19671-04c4-47ba-b00d-aa74d7685c16`) that carries the licenses you're selling. The seller generates it from their NI account and gives it to the buyer, who imports it into their own account.

NI's help pages don't mention any transfer fee for this process.

## Step by step: the seller

1. Log in to your **Native Instruments account**.
2. Go to **Profile → Products and licenses → Manage my licenses**.
3. Click **Transfer a license**.
4. Tick the products you're selling and click **Continue**.
5. Read the terms, agree and confirm.
6. You get a **Transfer ID**. Send it to the buyer, for example in your messages on Plugin Resale once you've been paid.

Before you do this, uninstall the products from your computers: once the transfer is done, they're no longer yours.

## Step by step: the buyer

1. Create a **Native Instruments account** if you don't have one.
2. Go to NI's **Import License** page and enter the Transfer ID.
3. The serial numbers are added to your account. Install and activate the products with Native Access.

Only confirm the deal once the products show up in your account and activate on your computer.

## What can't be transferred

NI lists a few reasons a product won't show up as transferable:

- **Recent registration.** Serial numbers are automatically blocked from transfer for **60 days after registration**.
- **NFR licenses** (Not For Resale), given for demos and reviews, can't be resold or transferred.
- **Educational (EDU) licenses** can't be resold or transferred.
- **Outdated versions of some products.** When a newer version of a bundle like Komplete Select comes out, the old version becomes non-transferable: only the current version can move.
- **Free titles included with other products**, like Massive X Player.
- **Third-party products** sold through NI: their transfers are handled by the company that made them.

NI's license terms also say that **bundles can only be resold as a whole**. If you own Komplete, you can't sell Kontakt on its own and keep the rest.

## Tips for sellers

- **Sell the bundle as one listing** and list the main instruments in your description, so buyers searching for them find it.
- **Check the 60-day rule** if you registered the product recently.
- **Mention the exact edition and version** (Komplete Standard, Ultimate or Collector's Edition, and which number): upgrading to the latest version costs money, and buyers will take it into account.

## Tips for buyers

- **Check the version** you're buying and what the upgrade to the current one would cost.
- **Compare with NI's own sales.** Komplete upgrades and crossgrades are often discounted, which sets a ceiling on what a used license is worth. Our [pricing guide](/blog/how-much-is-my-used-plugin-worth) explains the logic.
