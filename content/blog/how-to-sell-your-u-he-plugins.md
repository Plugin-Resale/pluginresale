u-he synths have a loyal following: Diva for the analog tone, Zebra for sound design, Hive for fast electronic work, Repro for the Prophet-style sounds. Many producers own more than one and end up using mostly the same favourite.

u-he allows transfers, with clear timing rules. Here's how it works.

## Can you resell a u-he license?

Generally, yes. u-he's own answer starts with "Generally yes, you can." The conditions depend on **how long ago you bought it**:

- **Less than 6 weeks after purchase:** transfers **aren't possible**.
- **Between 6 weeks and 9 months:** possible, with a **€15 transfer token** per license.
- **9 months or more** after purchase (or after the license was redeemed with a registration code): **free**.

What can't be transferred:

- **NFR (not for resale) licenses**, at all.
- **Soundsets on their own.** Soundsets move **automatically with their plug-in**, and don't need a token. It's not possible to transfer them separately, including The Dark Zebra.

## How much does it cost?

**Free after nine months.** Before that, **€15 per license**, paid with a transfer token. Tokens aren't listed in u-he's store: if you need one, u-he support sends you a link. The **current owner** has to buy the token, otherwise u-he can't assign it correctly. Don't buy one before contacting u-he about the transfer.

## How to transfer a u-he license, step by step

1. **Check the dates:** at least 6 weeks since purchase, and whether you're past 9 months.
2. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
3. **Request the transfer** through u-he's contact form ("Transfer your license") or at support@u-he.com.
4. **If u-he says a token is needed**, buy it through the link they send you, then tell them you've done it.
5. **u-he processes the transfer manually** and sends the license **directly to the buyer**.

Our database lists the delay as **processed manually by u-he support**: not instant, but handled by a person.

## A rule every buyer should know

u-he is very clear on this: **the buyer always receives the license (user name and serial number) directly from u-he, never from the seller.**

- If a seller sends you their user name and serial number, **decline the transfer**.
- If a seller sends you a registration code to enter on u-he's redeem page, **decline it too**: u-he says the code has most likely been obtained fraudulently.

As a seller, say in your listing that the transfer goes through u-he. It reassures buyers who know the rule.

## Traps to avoid

**Selling too soon.** Nothing is possible in the first six weeks, and before nine months it costs €15.

**Sending your serial.** That's exactly what u-he tells buyers to refuse.

**Selling an NFR license.** Not transferable.

**Selling a soundset alone.** It follows its plug-in.

## How to price a used u-he plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price they can find, or they'll just buy new.

Then adjust:

- **Past nine months, no fee:** you have more room on the price.
- **Mention soundsets** that come with the plug-in: they move with it and add value.
- **Name the exact product.** Zebra Legacy and Zebra 3, for example, are two different products in our catalogue.
