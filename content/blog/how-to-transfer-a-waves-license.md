Waves is probably the most traded plugin brand on the second-hand market. Plenty of producers own dozens of Waves plugins picked up during sales, and many of them never get used.

The good news: Waves has an official Transfer of Ownership program. The catch: your license has to meet a few conditions first, and one of them surprises a lot of sellers.

## Can you transfer a Waves license?

Yes, if all three of these are true:

1. **The product has been registered to your account for at least 3 months.**
2. **The license is version 9 or later.**
3. **The license is covered by an active Waves Update Plan (WUP).**

The third point is the one that catches people out. If your Update Plan has expired, Waves won't accept the transfer until it's covered again, which means renewing it first. Check your Waves account before you list a plugin for sale, and factor any renewal cost into your price.

Perpetual licenses can be transferred. **Waves Creative Access subscriptions can't.**

## How much does it cost?

Waves charges a transfer fee based on a percentage of the product's full list price, with a **minimum of $10 and a maximum of $150 per transfer**. The **seller** pays it, through a secure payment link Waves sends during the process.

## How to transfer a Waves license, step by step

1. **Check your licenses** in your Waves account: version 9 or later, and an active Update Plan.
2. **Deactivate the licenses** from all your computers and USB drives, in Waves Central.
3. **Fill in the transfer form** on the Waves website, using the email address your account is registered with, and the buyer's details.
4. **Waves checks** that everything is eligible.
5. **Pay the transfer fee** through the secure link Waves emails you.
6. **Waves confirms** the transfer by email. The licenses now appear in the buyer's account.

Waves says the process takes **up to 3 business days**.

The Update Plan moves with the license: the buyer gets the remaining coverage, and the end date doesn't change.

## Tips for buyers

- **Create your Waves account** before the deal, so the seller can give Waves your details right away.
- **Ask the seller when their Update Plan ends.** A license with a long coverage left is worth more than one about to expire.
- **Don't confirm the deal** until the plugins show up in your own Waves account and activate in Waves Central.

## Tips for sellers

- **Check the 3-month rule** if you bought the plugin recently.
- **Mention the Update Plan end date** in your listing. It's the first thing Waves buyers ask about.
- **Price it realistically.** Waves plugins go on sale very often, so buyers compare your price with the next sale, not the list price. Our [pricing guide](/blog/how-much-is-my-used-plugin-worth) explains how to find the right number.
