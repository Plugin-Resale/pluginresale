Serum is one of the best-known wavetable synths ever made, and Serum 2 brought it right back into the spotlight. So it's no surprise people search for "used Serum license" all the time, both to sell one and to buy one cheaper.

Here's the straight answer, and what it means for you.

## Can you resell a Serum license?

**No.** Xfer Records doesn't allow license transfers. This covers Serum and the other Xfer plugins in our catalogue: Cthulhu, LFOTool and Nerve.

According to our database, Xfer's position comes from **Steve Duda**, who has stated on Xfer's official forum since 2019 that licenses can't be transferred, citing **fraud concerns**.

That's why Xfer plugins can't be listed on Plugin Resale: we only list licenses the developer will actually move to the buyer.

## Why a "used Serum" is a risky buy

Since Xfer doesn't transfer licenses, anyone selling a "used Serum" is selling something Xfer won't put in your name. In practice, that usually means:

- **A shared login** to the seller's account. The seller keeps full control and can change the password, or the account can be closed.
- **A license file or serial** sent by message. It's still registered to the seller.

In both cases, you pay for something you don't own, and you have nothing to fall back on if it stops working. If a seller claims they can "transfer" a Serum license, ask how, and walk away if the answer isn't Xfer doing it.

## What about EU law?

In the EU, the law may allow the resale of software bought outright even when a license forbids it. But in practice, Xfer won't move the license to the buyer, so the buyer would have to go to court to use it. Our article on [whether you can legally resell your plugins](/blog/can-i-legally-resell-my-plugins) explains the legal side.

## What can you do with a Serum license you don't use?

Honestly, not much, officially:

- **Keep it.** Your license is still yours, and you may come back to it.
- **Check other plugins you own.** Most producers who own Serum also own plugins from developers that do allow transfers.
