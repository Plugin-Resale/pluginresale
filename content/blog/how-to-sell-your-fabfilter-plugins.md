FabFilter plugins are on a lot of mix templates: Pro-Q on every channel, Pro-L on the master bus, Saturn or Timeless when something needs character. They also tend to pile up. You bought the Mastering Bundle, then the Total Bundle, and now you own two licenses of the same Pro-L 2. Or you moved to Pro-Q 4 and your Pro-Q 3 license is sitting unused.

The good news: FabFilter has one of the simplest transfer policies around. Here's how to sell a FabFilter license properly, and what to watch out for.

## Can you resell a FabFilter license?

Yes. FabFilter allows you to transfer a license to another person, and the whole thing happens from your FabFilter account. No support ticket, no waiting for someone to approve it.

There are two exceptions:

- **Educational (EDU) and NFR licenses can't be transferred.** FabFilter treats educational licenses as Not For Resale. If you bought at a student price, you can't sell that license later.
- **Bundles can only be transferred as a whole.** If you own the Mixing Bundle, you can sell the bundle, but you can't split it and sell Pro-Q on its own while keeping the rest.

That second point matters when you plan a sale. If you only use two plugins out of a bundle, you can't keep them and sell the other ones. It's all or nothing.

## How much does it cost?

The seller buys a "License Transfer" in their FabFilter account. Our database puts it at **about €10**. FabFilter's FAQ doesn't print the amount, so check the exact price in your account before you agree on a price with the buyer.

The **seller** pays it. It's small next to the value of most FabFilter licenses, but include it in your numbers.

## How to transfer a FabFilter license, step by step

1. **Agree on the deal with the buyer** and get paid first. On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
2. **Ask the buyer for three things:** first name, last name and the email address they'll use for their FabFilter account.
3. **Buy a License Transfer** in your FabFilter account.
4. **Pick the license and transfer it** to the buyer's name and email, from the same account page.
5. **Uninstall the plugin** on your side. The license isn't yours anymore.

That's it. Our database lists the delay as **immediate once the seller does it**: there's no review step on FabFilter's side, so the buyer can have the license the same day.

FabFilter's FAQ also has a clear rule for buyers: if a seller refuses to go through this procedure, they're not selling a legit license. As a seller, that works for you. Tell the buyer upfront that you'll use the official transfer, and they'll trust the deal more.

## Traps to avoid

**Selling the wrong version.** At FabFilter, a new major version is a brand new plugin. Pro-Q 3 and Pro-Q 4 are two separate products, and so are Pro-C 2 and Pro-C 3. Your license gives you the major version you bought, plus free minor updates. Owners of the older version get an upgrade discount, not a free upgrade. So if you own Pro-Q 3, list Pro-Q 3, not Pro-Q 4. Put the exact version in your listing: a buyer who expects the latest one and gets the previous one has every reason to open a PayPal dispute.

**Forgetting what's in your bundle.** Before listing a bundle, check exactly which plugins and versions are in your license. List the bundle as one item.

**Selling an EDU license.** If there's any doubt, check your account. EDU licenses can't be transferred, so you'd be taking money for something the buyer will never get.

**Using PayPal Friends & Family.** Always Goods & Services. It protects the buyer, and a buyer who feels safe is a buyer who actually pays.

## How to price a used FabFilter plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today, not what you paid the day you bought it. Your used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price they can find, or they'll just buy it new.

Then adjust:

- **Older major version:** a Pro-Q 3 is worth less than a Pro-Q 4, since the buyer would pay again to upgrade.
- **Bundles:** price them against the bundle's own new price, not the sum of the plugins inside.
- **Transfer fee:** you pay about €10 for the transfer, so account for it in your minimum price.
