If you've bought plugins from Softube, Slate Digital, Sound Radix, Relab and many others, you already know iLok: the system that holds your licenses and lets your plugins run. When it's time to sell one of those plugins, iLok is also where the transfer happens.

Here's everything you need to know before you move an iLok license.

## What iLok is

iLok is a copy protection system made by a company called **PACE**. Your plugin licenses are stored in your **iLok account**, and you activate each one in one of three places:

- on an **iLok USB key**,
- on your **computer**,
- in **iLok Cloud** (the license stays online and needs an internet connection to run).

You manage all of this in **iLok License Manager**, a free app.

## How a transfer works

The seller sends the license from their iLok account to the buyer's iLok account, directly in iLok License Manager:

1. The **buyer** creates a free iLok account (if they don't have one) and gives the seller their **iLok User ID**.
2. The **seller** opens iLok License Manager, right-clicks the license and chooses **Transfer License**.
3. The seller enters the buyer's User ID, pays the fee by credit card and confirms.
4. The license appears in the buyer's iLok account, ready to activate.

It's usually done in minutes, as long as the developer allows it.

## How much does it cost?

PACE charges its own fee, on top of anything the developer charges:

- **$25** for a single license or bundle,
- **$50 in total** if you transfer several licenses or bundles in the same transaction.

So if you sell three iLok plugins to the same buyer, sending them together costs $50 instead of $75.

The fee is paid by credit card by whoever does the transfer, which means the seller. You can of course agree with the buyer to share it: just settle this before the sale.

Some developers add their own fee, others waive theirs and only the iLok fee applies. **iZotope**, for example, charges nothing itself, but the $25 iLok fee applies if the product was activated with iLok. You can check each developer in our [transfer rules database](/developers).

## The rules that block a transfer

### The developer decides

iLok only moves a license if the developer allows it. If iLok License Manager won't let you transfer a license, the developer has probably disabled transfers for that product, and PACE can't override that. Some developers also want to approve each transfer before it goes through.

### The 90-day rule

A license can't be transferred **within 90 days of being deposited** in your account, and you can't transfer from an **iLok account less than 90 days old**. If you just bought a plugin, you'll have to wait.

### Where the license is activated

If the license is activated on a computer, you have to do the transfer **from that computer**. If it's on an iLok USB key, the key must be **plugged into** the computer you're using.

## The mistake to avoid

**iLok transfers are irreversible.** If you type the wrong User ID, the license goes to someone else's account, and PACE can't bring it back.

To stay safe:

- ask the buyer to **copy and paste** their User ID in a message, rather than typing it from a screenshot,
- **double-check** it before you confirm, character by character,
- remember that a User ID isn't the same as an email address.

## After the transfer

- **Buyers**: activate the license on your computer, iLok USB or iLok Cloud, then check that the plugin loads before you confirm the deal. Some developers also need you to register the product in your account on their website: **Slate Digital**, for instance, asks buyers to contact its support so the product shows up in their Slate account.
- **Sellers**: remove the plugin from your computer, and keep the transfer receipt from PACE. It's your proof that the license left your account.
