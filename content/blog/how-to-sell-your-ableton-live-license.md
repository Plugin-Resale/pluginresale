Maybe you moved to another DAW. Maybe you upgraded from Standard to Suite and kept the old license around. Or you bought Live with a Push and never really used the software. Whatever the reason, an unused Ableton Live license is worth something to someone.

Ableton has one of the cleanest transfer processes of any software maker. Here's how it works.

## Can you resell an Ableton Live license?

Yes. Ableton lets you transfer a Live, Push or Move license to another person, directly from your Ableton account. It's **free**, and our database lists it as **immediate** (self-service).

A few rules:

- **Only entire Live licenses can be transferred.** All the serial numbers in the license go to the buyer, including any upgraded serials. You can't keep part of it.
- **Educational licenses** can only be transferred to an **eligible student or teacher**. The buyer will be asked for verification to activate it.
- **NFR (Not for Resale) licenses can't be transferred.**
- **The request must come from the seller's registered email address.**

## How to transfer an Ableton Live license, step by step

1. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
2. **Ask for the buyer's email address** registered with ableton.com. They need an Ableton account.
3. **Log in to your Ableton account** and click **Transfer ownership**.
4. **Enter the buyer's email**, choose the license and click **Submit**.
5. **Confirm by email:** Ableton sends you a confirmation email. Click **Confirm product transfer** (check your spam folder if it doesn't show up).
6. **Confirm on ableton.com** when you're redirected there.
7. **Both of you get a final email** once the license has moved.

## Selling Live with a Push or Move

If you're selling Ableton hardware, Ableton says any license included in the second-hand hardware sale should be **agreed privately between the two parties**. If a **Live license** should go with the Push or Move serial number, it has to be **explicitly requested** when the seller contacts support.

## A warning for buyers

Ableton is open about one risk: in rare cases, a seller bought their copy of Live with a fraudulent credit card. When Ableton finds out, it **disables the license, even if it has already been resold** to an unsuspecting buyer.

That's why a proper transfer through Ableton's system, from a seller with reviews, matters. And why Plugin Resale asks buyers to pay with PayPal Goods & Services, which protects them.

## Traps to avoid

**Trying to keep part of the license.** An upgraded license moves as a whole, with every serial in it.

**Selling an education license to anyone.** Only eligible students or teachers can receive it.

**Transferring from another email.** The request has to come from the address registered on your Ableton account.

**Selling a Push with a license you meant to keep.** Say clearly in your listing whether a Live license is included.

## How to price a used Ableton Live license

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price they can find, or they'll just buy it new.

Then adjust:

- **Edition and version:** Suite, Standard or Intro, and the version number. List them exactly.
- **No fee to cover:** the transfer is free.
