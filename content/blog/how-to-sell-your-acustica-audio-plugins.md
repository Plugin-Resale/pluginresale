Acustica Audio users tend to collect. One channel strip becomes three, a Volume A turns into a whole series, and a few years later the Aquarius library is huge. Some of those plugins end up never loaded.

The good news: Acustica has one of the most seller-friendly transfer systems around. It's automated, and usually free. Here's how it works.

## Can you resell an Acustica Audio license?

Yes. Acustica allows product transfers between accounts, done **entirely through its Aquarius web service**, with no helpdesk involved.

The conditions:

- **The product must have been in your account for at least 180 days.**
- **You must de-authorize and uninstall the product** before transferring it.
- **Some license types can't be transferred:** time-limited, educational, NFR, special, OEM, institutional and retail-bundled licenses.

## How much does it cost?

Acustica works with transfer counters:

- **Each product can be transferred five times for free.** After five transfers, it can't be moved through the free system anymore.
- **Extra transfers** can be requested at a cost of **€25 plus the difference between the product's purchase price and its retail price**.

For most sellers, the transfer is free. In our database, the **seller** starts the transfer.

## How to transfer an Acustica Audio license, step by step

1. **Check the 180-day rule.** If you bought the plugin less than six months ago, wait.
2. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
3. **Ask the buyer for their Acustica Audio account.** They need one to receive the product.
4. **De-authorize and uninstall** the product on your side.
5. **Start the transfer in Aquarius.** The process is automatic, and our database lists the transfer as **immediate**.
6. **Check with the buyer** that the product shows up in their account and authorizes.

## Traps to avoid

**Selling too early.** Under 180 days, the transfer won't go through. Don't list a plugin you just bought.

**Selling an excluded license.** Educational, NFR, OEM, special and retail-bundled licenses can't move. If you got a plugin free with something else, check its license type first.

**Forgetting to de-authorize.** Acustica makes it mandatory. Do it before you start the transfer.

**Ignoring the transfer counter.** A product that's already been resold a few times gets closer to its five-transfer limit. A buyer who plans to resell later may ask.

## How to price a used Acustica plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today, not what you paid. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price the buyer can find.

Then adjust:

- **No fee to cover** in most cases, since the first five transfers are free. That gives you a bit more room on price than with developers who charge per transfer.
- **Name the exact product and version.** Acustica has many close names and volumes (A, B, C...). Be precise so the buyer knows exactly what they get.
- **Bundles:** bundled licenses can't be split. If your plugin came as part of a retail bundle, it can't be sold alone.
