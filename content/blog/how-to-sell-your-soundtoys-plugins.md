Decapitator on the drum bus, EchoBoy on a vocal throw, Little AlterBoy for the weird stuff: Soundtoys plugins end up in a lot of sessions. They also end up duplicated. Maybe you bought EchoBoy on its own years ago, then picked up the Soundtoys 5 bundle, and now you own the same plugin twice.

That spare license can go to someone who needs it. Here's how a Soundtoys transfer works, and what to check before you list one.

## Can you resell a Soundtoys license?

Yes, with one condition worth reading twice. Soundtoys' license says you can't transfer it to a third party **except with Soundtoys' express written consent**. In practice, Soundtoys publishes a transfer procedure, but it keeps the right to refuse a request.

So a Soundtoys transfer isn't fully automatic. You move the license on iLok, then Soundtoys updates its records. Keep that in mind when you promise a buyer a timeline.

Soundtoys also says it won't support or allow upgrades on fraudulently obtained or stolen licenses. If you bought from Soundtoys or an authorized reseller, you're fine.

## How much does it cost?

Soundtoys doesn't charge its own fee. The cost is the **$25 iLok transfer fee**, charged by PACE (the company behind iLok) for each license transfer. The **seller** pays it, inside iLok License Manager.

## How to transfer a Soundtoys license, step by step

1. **Agree on the deal and get paid.** On Plugin Resale, the buyer pays you directly with PayPal Goods & Services.
2. **Get the buyer's iLok User ID** and the email address they use with Soundtoys. If they don't have an iLok account yet, they create one for free.
3. **Deactivate the license** on your computers or iLok key.
4. **Open iLok License Manager**, select the license and transfer it to the buyer's iLok User ID. Pay the $25 fee there.
5. **Email Soundtoys support** with the buyer's email address and iLok User ID, so Soundtoys can update the registration.
6. **Uninstall the plugin** on your side.

Soundtoys doesn't publish a typical delay. The iLok part happens as soon as you do it. The registration update depends on Soundtoys support.

## Traps to avoid

**Skipping the email to Soundtoys.** The iLok transfer moves the license, but Soundtoys' own records still show you as the owner until you tell them. Send the email, and forward the reply to the buyer.

**Sending a serial number instead of an iLok transfer.** A Soundtoys license is an iLok license. A buyer should get it in their own iLok account, not as a code in a message.

**Forgetting the fee in your price.** $25 on a cheap plugin is a big share of the price. On a single small effect, it can eat most of the discount you're offering.

**Promising a date you don't control.** Since Soundtoys can refuse a transfer, don't tell the buyer "done by tonight". Say the iLok part is instant and the registration update is up to Soundtoys.

## How to price a used Soundtoys plugin

Start from what the buyer would pay for it new today, not the price you paid back then. A used license has to be clearly cheaper than the best new price the buyer can find, or they'll just buy it new.

Then adjust:

- **Add the iLok fee to your math.** You pay $25 per license, so it sets a floor on how low you can go.
- **Single plugin vs bundle.** A bundle like Soundtoys 5 is priced against the bundle's own new price, not the sum of the plugins inside it.
- **Be clear about the version** in your listing, so the buyer knows exactly what they're getting.
