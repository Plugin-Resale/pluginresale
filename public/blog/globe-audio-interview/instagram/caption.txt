Interview: Globe Audio Mastering (@globeaudiomastering) 🎚️

Alexis Bardinet (@alexis.bardinet) and Lorenzo Bassaletti (@lorenzo_bassaletti), mastering engineers in Bordeaux. Globe Audio has been mastering records since 1999 (Youssou N'Dour, Arthur H, Francis Cabrel, SCH, Salif Keita, Ibrahim Maalouf, Katia & Marielle Labèque, the soundtracks of Les Misérables and La Tortue Rouge, many Gold, Platinum and Diamond records), mostly in the room with the artist, on a chain of 15 to 20 machines.

We talked about mastering from DAT tapes to streaming, working with artists in the room, custom-modified gear, loudness, what makes a mix hard to master, and the few plugins they keep next to the analog chain: swipe →
